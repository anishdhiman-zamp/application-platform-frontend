# Medium Integration Guide

Connect your Medium account to track article performance, analyze readership, and manage your publication data.

## Prerequisites

- A Medium account
- Published articles or a publication

## Setup Instructions

### Step 1: Generate Integration Token

1. Go to Medium **Settings** → **Security and apps**
2. Scroll to **Integration tokens**
3. Click **Get integration token**
4. Copy the generated token

### Step 2: Connect to Zamp

1. Navigate to **Integrations** → **Medium**
2. Click **Connect**
3. Paste your integration token
4. Click **Verify & Connect**

### Step 3: Select Publications

Choose what to sync:

- Personal stories
- Publication stories (if you're a writer/editor)
- Stats and analytics

## Available Data

### Article Metrics

- 👁️ Views and reads
- 👏 Claps and fans
- 📊 Read ratio
- 💬 Responses

### Publication Stats

- Subscriber count
- Top performing articles
- Traffic sources
- Follower growth

### Content Data

- Article titles and URLs
- Publication dates
- Tags and topics
- Word count

## Analytics Dashboard

Once connected, you'll have access to:

```
📈 Performance Trends
├── Views over time
├── Engagement rates
├── Best posting times
└── Content performance
```

## Use Cases

### Content Strategy

- Identify top-performing topics
- Optimize posting schedule
- Track audience growth

### Reporting

- Generate publication reports
- Share metrics with stakeholders
- Export data for analysis

## Limitations

> ℹ️ Medium's API has rate limits. Data syncs every 6 hours to ensure compliance and reliability.
